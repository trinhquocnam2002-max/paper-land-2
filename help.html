Frequently asked questions

Orders & shipping

How much is shipping?
Standard shipping is $9.95 anywhere in Australia, and free on orders over $60. Orders are dispatched from Adelaide within 1–2 business days and usually arrive in 2–7 business days.
Do you ship internationally?
Not yet — we currently ship within Australia only. Join the Paper Club to hear when that changes.
Can I return or exchange an item?
Unused items in original condition can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Contact us with your order number and we'll send return instructions. Faulty items are always replaced free of charge.

Our notebooks

What pens work best?
Our 120gsm paper is tested with gel pens, ballpoints, pencils and fine fountain pens. Very wet markers may show through, so test on the last page first.
What's the difference between dot grid, lined and plain?
Dot grid is the most flexible — great for bullet journaling and layouts. Lined is best for long-form writing. Plain is for sketching and lettering.
Is the planner dated?
No. The Someday planner is undated, so you can start in any month and skip weeks without wasting pages.

Gifts & wholesale

Do you offer gift wrapping?
Our gift sets come in a kraft gift box. For single notebooks, add a note at checkout and we'll include a handwritten card.
Can my shop stock Paper Land?
Yes — we'd love to hear from independent stationery, gift and book shops. Choose "Wholesale" in the contact form.
