Our Story

One word,
one blank page.

Designed in Adelaide · Inspired by Saigon

Notebooks for the small rituals.

Paper Land began with a simple habit: sitting down each morning with a coffee and a notebook to write out the day. It is a ritual from the cafés of Saigon that travelled well to Adelaide — different coffee, same feeling.

We design notebooks for that habit. Instead of a big logo, each cover carries one everyday word and a short, honest definition. Words like arvo, cuppa and iced coffee — the small things that make an ordinary day feel like yours.

Everything is designed in Adelaide, with paper and binding chosen to be written in daily, not saved for best.
